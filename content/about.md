The Community Crowdfunding System (CCS) is how the Monero community funds work that benefits
Monero: development, research, events and more.

1. **Idea.** Anyone can propose something by opening a merge request on the
   [proposals repository](https://repo.getmonero.org/monero-project/ccs-proposals), where the
   community discusses it.
2. **Funding Required.** Once approved and merged, the proposal gets its own donation address
   and anyone can contribute.
3. **Work in Progress.** Fully funded, the work starts; funds are paid out per milestone.
4. **Completed.** Every milestone is met and paid out.

This site is static HTML: no JavaScript, no tracking, and it works over Tor.
